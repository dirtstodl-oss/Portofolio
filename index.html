Background

How I got here, and where I'm headed

How I Learn

I started learning self-taught in 2021 purely as a hobby—taking things apart, breaking projects on purpose to fix them, and mastering the fundamentals through trial and error without releasing any games. By 2025, I transitioned to pursuing game development intensively as my primary career.

    What I'm Planning

    Over the next few years, I plan to transition from a solo builder into a lead environment artist and studio founder. My goal is to build an independent, self-sustaining game studio where I can lead a dedicated team to create successful Roblox experiences—shifting from working for a paycheck to owning and scaling high-impact titles together.
